name: 60 fps
description: The game at 60 frames a second and its normal speed (50 on a PAL 50 Hz screen): the frame time, the Flash menus and HUD, physics, ragdolls, weapon recoil, sway and zoom, and breaking glass all keep the speed they have at 30. Locked: a scene the device runs below 60 plays slower.
author: Press5elect

# BLACK (US, 45410083 v2). Copyright (c) 2026 Press5elect: any use of this mod
# or its source, including through AI tools, requires credit to Press5elect.
# Timing taken from TGP482/BLACK-XBERecomp (src/game/hooks.cpp), its
# FrameShare (frame time / 1/30 s) fixed at 0.5.
# v2: the code is in the int3 padding after functions inside .text (124
# pieces linked by jumps); v1 ran it from the header page, which crashed the
# PS5 native CPU core when a weapon's sway first ran. The header is untouched.
# The 30 fps limiter is the device's presentation interval, TWO_OR_IMMEDIATE
# (0x1783ED): now ONE_OR_IMMEDIATE. The level start sets the game's frame
# time (float 0x2F3A38) to 1/30 (1/25 PAL, 0x20C282): now 1/60 (1/50). Its
# timers, the game clock (physics step and gravity at a level's start, films)
# and the Flash tick (frame time * 1000 ms, now * 1020: 17 ms) follow it.
# What steps a constant a frame is halved, square rooted or run every other
# frame (the frame counter 0x2F4368's parity): recoil, gun sway, aim zoom,
# move ease, the reload blur's hold, physics damping, top speed, sleep limit
# and count, joint limits, contact reach, ragdoll look ahead, glass shards and
# the glass sound slots. Source: black_60fps_caves.asm, black_60fps_patch.py.
