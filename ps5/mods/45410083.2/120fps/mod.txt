name: 120 fps
description: The game at 120 frames a second and its normal speed (100 on a PAL 50 Hz screen), for a 120 Hz screen (Settings > Graphics > Display refresh rate: 120 Hz): the frame time, the Flash menus and HUD, physics, ragdolls, weapon recoil, sway and zoom, and breaking glass all keep the speed they have at 30. It needs a lot more of the console: lower the internal resolution if it can't keep up (locked: a scene the device runs below 120 plays slower). Turn the 60 fps mod off to use it.
author: Press5elect
refresh: 120

# (src/game/hooks.cpp, config/game.toml), its FrameShare (frame time / 1/30 s) fixed at 0.25.
# The console's vblank at 120 Hz (refresh: 120) and the present interval ONE_OR_IMMEDIATE
# (0x1783ED): a frame each vblank. The level start sets the game's frame time (float
# 0x2F3A38) to 1/30 (1/25 PAL, 0x20C282): now 1/120 (1/100), its ms 8 (10). Its timers, the
# game clock (physics step and gravity at a level's start, films) and the Flash tick follow
# it. What steps a constant a frame is scaled by 0.25 (halved in the 60 fps mod), taken to the
# fourth root (square rooted: here sqrt twice) or run every fourth frame (every other: the
# frame counter 0x2F4368's low two bits): recoil, gun sway, aim zoom, move ease, the reload
# blur's hold, physics damping, top speed, sleep limit (x 1/16) and count, joint limits, contact
# reach, ragdoll look ahead, glass shards and the glass sound slots; a joint's anchor gap is put
# right by a quarter a frame (motion + (gap - motion) / 4), a shard's turn by a quarter. The
# Flash tick is 9 ms on one frame in four and 8 on the others (8.25 ms a 1/120 frame, the
# recomp's carried real time 8.33). XMV movies count vblanks at the display mode's rate (60):
# their ms a vblank halved for the 120 Hz vblank. The code is in the int3 padding after
# functions inside .text, as the 60 fps mod's (its pieces, 10 more).
