name: 120 fps
description: The game at 120 frames a second, for a 120 Hz screen (Settings > Graphics > Display refresh rate: 120 Hz). The game keeps its normal speed, and its menus, loading screens and videos keep theirs. It needs a lot more of the console, so lower the internal resolution if it can't keep up. Turn the 60 fps mod off to use it.
author: Press5elect
refresh: 120
