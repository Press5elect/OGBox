name: Smooth frame pacing
description: The game's motion was uneven at 60 frames a second: some frames moved the action twice and others not at all. It now moves one step every frame (two in the 30 fps cut scenes), at the game's normal speed.

# The main loop (0x11CD0) runs fixed 1/60 s steps (0x2C530 returns the constant 1/60). It started
# a 60.000 Hz timer (0xD7560 -> 0xD7DA0: D3D's timer callback every QPF/60 = 12222222 ticks) whose
# callback (0xD7EA0) wakes the input thread (0xD7700), which queues one pad sample a tick (a queue
# of 10, 0xD7AE0). Each frame, NextStep (0xD75E0) ran one step per sample queued since the last
# frame (5 at most). The frames come at the vblank (59.94 Hz, interval ONE), the samples at 60 Hz
# by a separate clock: when a tick fell near the moment the loop looked (on hakuX the vblank is
# locked to the panel and the timer interrupt arrives 1-2 ms, now and then 10+ ms, late), frames
# got 0 or 2 steps in turn: the same picture twice, then a jump (8 % of frames with 1 ms of jitter,
# 16 % with 2, in long runs at 59.94 Hz; up to every frame at a bad phase).
# NextStep is rewritten in place (its only callers are the loop, 0x11D4C and 0x11D97): the steps
# now come from the time (QueryPerformanceCounter 0x1F5369, the same clock and period), counted
# once a frame: floor(time not yet stepped / step), the rest carried, at most 5 as before; a
# frame that would get one step less than its length expects (1, or 2 for a frame of 1.75 steps
# or more) gets it once half a step is due, and the rest is then not kept below 0. So one step a
# frame at 60 (two at 30), an extra one every ~17 s at 59.94 Hz, the speed as before. Each step
# takes the queued pad samples (the newest last, by the queue's own pop 0xD7AA0 under its lock),
# or keeps the last one when none came. V (this+0x1A54, a flag that was always 0) and the last
# frame's time (this+0x1A58, a byte only this function read) are its state.
