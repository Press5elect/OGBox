name: 60 fps
description: The game at 60 frames a second instead of 30 (50 instead of 25 in 50 Hz; turn PAL 60 Hz on in Settings > Video for 60). Its clock already measures each frame's real time (as on PC), so it keeps its normal speed. Like the PC game above 30 fps, a few things may act differently (some physics and animations); turn it off if something goes wrong.
author: Press5elect

# The frame limiter (0x2738A3, before each flip while the game is playing, state 7) waits
# until the vblank count (0x5F27D4) is two past the count at the
# last flip: 30 fps (25 in 50 Hz). Its second wait (count == last + 1) goes, so one vblank
# is enough. CTimer::Update takes the frame's time from the CPU's counter
# (733333 ticks a ms) and the step as ms x 0.05, clamped: nothing is per frame.
[default.xbe]
0x002738BC: 39 0D D4 27 5F 00 74 ED -> 39 0D D4 27 5F 00 90 90
