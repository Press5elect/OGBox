name: 120 fps
description: The game at 120 frames a second, for a 120 Hz screen (Settings > Graphics > Display refresh rate: 120 Hz). The game keeps its normal speed and the videos their own; it needs a lot more of the console, so lower the internal resolution if it can't keep up. It has the 60 FPS patch's changes in it: turn the 60 FPS patch off to use it.
author: Press5elect
refresh: 120
