## Added
- **Hard disk size** (Settings > Xbox): what size the emulated Xbox hard disk reports to games.
  - **Automatic** (the default): E: (saves and downloads) is as large as what it holds, plus the
    free space of an empty Xbox hard disk (about 4.9 GB), and never more than your storage has free.
  - **8 GB** is the Xbox's own layout, as before.
  - **16, 32, 64 or 128 GB** give E: all the space beyond the other partitions. A fixed size can
    leave E: full, and a large one shows games more free space than an Xbox ever had.

## Fixed
- E: was reported as a full drive once it held more than an Xbox's 4.9 GB, so games found no free
  space for saves. Automatic hard disk size fixes it.
- Stencil shadows that were missing (`45410012`). Shadow volumes that count in the stencil buffer
  without writing depth now use the GPU's own depth, not exact depth's.
- White streaks across the picture (`4D5300D1`), there since the first public builds. Exact depth
  now keeps the game's own depth only for primitives that lie at one depth, which is what it was
  made for (a sky that flickered), and uses the GPU's depth for everything else.
- The kernel now has all 337 of the Xbox kernel's functions. The last ones are:
  - the driver model: I/O requests, drivers and device queues;
  - kernel calls that run several of the game's own routines in turn;
  - thread notify routines, `PsCreateSystemThread` and `PsQueryStatistics`;
  - interlocked lists, HAL interrupt masks and software interrupts;
  - the runtime library's string, number and `printf` functions.
- Merged from hakuX-lr (PortareOS):
  - kernel:
    - priority boosts after waits, as the Xbox kernel gives them;
    - `KeQuerySystemTime` in the game's own code;
    - the processor's MTRR capabilities as a Pentium III reports them;
    - unbuffered overlapped hard disk transfers;
    - the top 128 KiB of RAM reserved at boot;
    - RAM reached through large pages;
    - EEPROM reads and writes over SMBus;
  - GPU:
    - logic ops;
    - register-combiner precision;
    - point sizes;
    - specular colour and alpha;
    - GL-style clamping and texture LOD limits;
    - context DMA bases;
    - P8 palettes;
    - the NV039 notifier;
    - `R16B16` textures and `X1R5G5B5` clears;
    - methods skipped while no graphics class is bound;
    - wireframe and point fill modes, and smoothing;
    - the PVIDEO overlay;
    - cylindrical texture wrap (off by default);
  - sound:
    - a sound no longer ends a frame early;
    - save states keep each voice's modulation and filter state.

## Tested
On a PS5, from test builds of this version:
- `45410012`: its shadows right.
- `4D5300D1`: no white streaks.
- Frame rates in play, each within 2 fps of earlier builds:
  - `45410012`: 59.1 to 59.9 fps;
  - `4D5300D1`: 57.9 to 59.9 fps;
  - `41560020`: 59.7 fps;
  - `53450004`: 59.9 fps;
  - `54540082` at 120 Hz: 118.5 fps;
  - `55530041` at 120 Hz: 119.9 fps.
- Hard disk size: Automatic.
- The sound, on this release's mix (1.0.0's), in `53450004` (a median of 59.9 fps), `54540082` at
  120 Hz (118.6) and `41560020` (59.8), every run ending normally.
- On the PC: 30 of 30 host tests, including new ones for the driver model and the `printf` family.

## Installation
1. Download `OGBox-v1.0.1.zip` below and check it against `OGBox-v1.0.1.zip.sha256`.
2. Copy the `PPSA99251` folder in it to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, replacing the old one.
   0.0.2 and newer also offer this release at their next start (Settings > Updates). Your
   settings, saves, save states and caches stay where they are (`/data/ogbox`, or the data folder
   you chose).
3. Put your own disc images (or extracted games) on the console, a USB drive, extended storage or
   a network share. Read `LEGAL.txt` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
Open an issue on this repository and include:
- `ogbox-boot.log` and `ogbox-boot.previous.log` from the title folder (copy them before you
  start OGBox again);
- your firmware version;
- what you did.

If the problem isn't a crash, choose **Save the Log** in the quick menu first. Never attach or
link game files, BIOS files or keys.
