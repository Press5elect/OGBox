## Added
- **Picture options** (Settings > Picture, per game; off by default and experimental):
  - **Texture upscaling** (2x or 4x): a texture that stays the same for a second is drawn again at
    2 or 4 times its size with an edge-keeping filter. Text, masks, normal maps and pixel-art
    textures stay as they are. **Upscaled texture format**: Compressed (the default) keeps an
    upscaled compressed (DXT) texture compressed, as the original is, so the GPU reads 4 to 8 times
    less memory for it; Uncompressed stores it in full.
  - **Foliage coverage**: leaves, grass and fences that thin out in the distance stay as full as up
    close.
  - **Shadow softness**: softer shadow-map edges (Soft, Soft wide, Softer). Game's default is the
    Xbox's own filter.
  - **Per-pixel lighting** for games drawn with fixed-function lights.
  - **Ambient occlusion**, with its strength and radius, and for games drawn with vertex programs
    the camera's near and far planes and field of view (without them it stays off for those games).
  - **FSR1's sharpening** (RCAS) after FSR1's upscale, with its strength.
  - On by default: smoother small glow effects at high resolutions, and Texture sharpness applied
    to colour textures only.
- **Speed limit** (Settings > Performance, per game or for all): 100% to 300% of the Xbox's speed,
  or Unlimited.
- **Your own patches**: `.jmp`, `.ips` and `.bps` files in the data folder's `patches` folder
  (`/data/ogbox/patches` by default; one level of folders is fine). OGBox matches each to its game
  by the checksum or title ID it carries, or by the title ID or name in its file name, and lists it
  under the game's Game Patches, off until you turn it on. A file it can't match can be attached to
  a game by hand.
- **Fast writes on the console's storage** (Settings > Advanced, on by default): the cache drives
  are written through a helper that isn't held to the title's write budget, and moving the data
  folder onto the console's storage copies through it.
- **Updates**: the version an update replaced is kept, and you can go back to it; the offer shows
  what's new since your version, and can skip a version.
- **A loading screen** while a game starts: its cover, its name and the steps of its start.
- **Settings > Interface > Accessibility**: larger text, high contrast and reduced motion.
- After a crash, the next start says that OGBox stopped and where its report is.
- OgXHD's widescreen patches for any release of 30 more games (they were for one release only).
- Settings > Advanced > Diagnostics: **One GPU device** and **Guest RAM imported**, both off.

## Changed
- Cheats are downloaded for the games in your library only, eight files at once, and a game's page
  fetches its own.
- With two GPU devices (the default), the screen no longer waits for each of the game's frames, and
  no game frame is dropped while the GPU is busy.

## Fixed
- A crash at a game's start with framebuffer fetch turned off.

## Tested
On a PS5, from test builds of this version:
- `55530041` (v2) at 4x resolution and 120 Hz, in one spot:
  - Texture upscaling at 2x with Soft wide shadows and foliage coverage: 120 frames a second
    (test build FE). At 4x, Soft wide and Softer ran at the same speed (112 frames a second each;
    the upscaling is the cost there).
  - Upscaled textures kept compressed: 52.7 MiB of upscaled images at 4x against 351 MiB
    uncompressed, and 116 to 121 frames a second with ambient occlusion on (test build FF).
  - The frame handoff: every frame the game made was shown, 1,199 of 1,199 in each 10 seconds for
    about two minutes, with no refresh skipped (test build FG; before it, 920 to 970 of 1,200).
  - Ambient occlusion costs about 10 frames a second at 120 Hz in this game.
- `45410083` (v2) at 6x with texture upscaling and per-pixel lighting: 120 frames a second in most
  10-second windows (test build FE).
- The write helper on the console's storage: about 200 MiB/s past 2 GiB, where the title's own
  writes fell to 12 to 32 MiB/s after its first 1.3 GiB (test build EV).
- On the PC: every PC test (`tests/run-pc-tests.sh --fetch`), among them the frame handoff's and
  the storage tests; hakuX-native's tests (`tests/hle`, its 36 test programs, with the new block
  encoder's; the block encoder's GLSL against its C on lavapipe, `tests/texbc`; `tests/cohalias`;
  `tests/coh16k`; `tests/vkshare`).

Not specifically tested on the console: the data folder move through the helper, the cache
drives' first copy through it, the speed limit, your own patches, the cheats download for your
games, the updater's going back and skipping, the loading screen, Accessibility, the crash notice,
and the new widescreen patches.

## Installation
1. Download `OGBox-v1.0.4.zip` below and check it against `OGBox-v1.0.4.zip.sha256`.
2. Copy the `PPSA99251` folder in it to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, replacing the old one.
   0.0.2 and newer also offer this release at their next start (Settings > Updates). Your
   settings, saves, save states and caches stay where they are (`/data/ogbox`, or the data folder
   you chose).
3. Put your own disc images (or extracted games) on the console, a USB drive, extended storage or
   a network share. Read `LEGAL.txt` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
Open an issue on this repository. Before you report a game's problem, please:
1. Use a dump of your own disc that matches Redump (the names in OGBox's game database are
   Redump's). A bad or modified dump causes faults OGBox can't fix.
2. Try the game with every mod, cheat or trainer and XBE patch turned off (and default settings,
   if you can), and say whether it still happens.
3. Include:
   - `ogbox-boot.log` from the title folder (choose **Save the Log** in the quick menu first if it
     wasn't a crash; after a crash, copy it and `ogbox-boot.previous.log` before you start OGBox
     again);
   - the game's **Title ID** as its page shows it (with the version after the dot, as in
     `4D530039.1`);
   - your OGBox version and your firmware version;
   - what you did.

Never attach or link game files, BIOS files or keys.
