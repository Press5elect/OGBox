# OGBox v0.0.5

An unofficial port of the hakuX original Xbox emulator to the PS5, as a homebrew title
(`PPSA99251`) for a jailbroken console.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Piracy is not condoned.** This release contains no games, no Xbox BIOS, boot ROM, EEPROM,
> firmware or keys, and none will ever be provided or linked to. Use only **backups of games you
> own**, made yourself from your own discs.

> [!WARNING]
> **Highly experimental.** Expect crashes and freezes. This is my build of hakuX for the PS5, not
> the hakuX project's: please report problems here, not to hakuX.

## Added
- Settings > Controller: which DualSense button is each Xbox button, trigger, D-pad direction,
  Start, Back and stick click; swapped sticks, inverted up and down, and deadzones for each stick
  and the triggers. A game can have its own, and changes apply at once, in a game too.
- Game descriptions on Home, in both layouts.

## Changed
- One look for every menu: a cooler dark background, the Xbox green as the only accent and one
  highlight. Home has a strip for the folders, the dashboard and the actions above the games, the
  chosen game shown large and a row of covers below.
- The quick menu, the mods screen, the data folder, the hard disk, the notice, the boot screen
  and the in-game status are rebuilt in that look.
- Covers download three at a time; a folder's descriptions are fetched in the background.

## Fixed
- Quick taps on the controller that never reached the game: a press is now held for at least
  50 ms.
- Reading damaged or malicious BPS and IPS patches, patch records and title images could read or
  write outside their buffers.
- The game page's actions running under the footer when a game had all six.

## Installation
1. Download `OGBox-v0.0.5.zip` below and check it against `OGBox-v0.0.5.zip.sha256`.
2. Copy the `PPSA99251` folder in it to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, replacing the old one.
   0.0.2 and newer also offer this release at its next start (Settings > Updates). Your settings, saves,
   save states and caches stay (`/data/ogbox`, or the data folder you chose).
3. Put your own disc images on the console, a USB drive, extended storage or a network share, and
   read `LEGAL.txt` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
Open an issue on this repository with `ogbox-boot.log` and `ogbox-boot.previous.log` from the
title folder (copy them before you start OGBox again), your firmware version and what you did.
Never attach or link game files, BIOS files or keys.
