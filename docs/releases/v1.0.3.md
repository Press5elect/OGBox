## Added
- **Download cheats** (Settings > Updates): the Xbox scene's trainers (`.etm` and `.xbtf`) that
  xbased keeps on GitHub, downloaded at a pinned commit of its repository into the data folder's
  `trainers` (`trainers/<title ID>/`), only when you choose it. Each file is checked against its
  size and SHA-256; a file already there is kept as it is, and nothing is deleted. OGBox ships only
  the index (the files' paths, sizes and hashes): the trainers are third-party files, made by the
  Xbox scene's trainer makers, for games you own.
- **HRTF** (Settings > Advanced, also per game): the filters the Xbox puts on 3D voices so their
  direction can be heard on headphones. On by default; off gives a plain pan.
- **Covers from MobCat's database** by the disc's title ID and version: the exact release's box,
  the plain box of its region before a Platinum or Classics one, and the database's thumbnail where
  it has no scan.
- A mod for `4156002A` (v1 and v3), after xbased (crazyriddler): animated water and lights (the
  world's animated textures move).
- The hard disk manager's copies, moves and deletions run in the background, with their progress
  at the foot of the screen; a data folder move shows its "deleting the old copy" phase.

## Fixed
### Games
- `54540079`: froze in races. A compare instruction (`COMISS`) the emulator couldn't take on a
  watched page faulted again and again; it is now emulated, and the game's writes beside a watched
  page count towards making that page hot.
- `4D530004` and `4D53000D`: mipmapped textures drawn from render targets each frame sampled only
  their first level, and were scaled up while their targets weren't (water reflections, hair, light
  and the pond). They now sample copies of every level, at the targets' resolution.
- `5553001D`: a halo or glow outline round characters and objects at high internal resolutions
  (small glow and blur targets now render at 1x), and a band of stray colour down the right of the
  screen.
- `4D530039`: cars white for good after several stages. The cube reflections of earlier stages are
  freed when the object table is full.
- `49470004`: dark squares instead of shadows on the snow (F24 depth shadow buffers compared at
  their own range).
- Fixes ported from xbased (crazyriddler), by the title ID each was made for: `49470007`,
  `4947001F`, `534E0007`, `42560004`, `42530009`, `53550007`, `4B4F0004`, `4541006D` (kernel and
  disc I/O); `4C41000D` (memory); `534300FD`, `54430006`, `45410015`, `4E4D0015`, `5553003B`,
  `55530058`, `5553004B`, `544D0003`, `544D000D`, `49470038`, `5655002D`, `534300FA`, `4541003E`,
  `5454000F` (graphics); `454D0003` and `56550013` (their first video's read).
- After HakuXP's records (MobCat): `4D440003` stopped after its nickname screen (a yield now gives
  way to a ready thread of a lower priority); `\??\CdRom0:` paths reach the disc.

### The emulator
- A crash of the game's own code (a page fault it doesn't handle) stops the game with a message
  instead of closing OGBox.
- Save states: the clean-up of the cache copies after each save deleted every copy, its own state's
  included, so a state could fail to load once the game had rewritten its cache. States saved with
  1.0.2 may have lost their copies; such a state now loads when the game's cache file still holds
  what it held when the state was saved.
- A cache drive's format empties its folder at once and deletes the old files in the background,
  instead of stopping the game while they are deleted.

### The front end
- A data folder move finishes visibly, deletes the old copy only once the new folder is recorded,
  refuses a target inside the data folder, and the hard disk manager and the save sync follow the
  new folder.
- Crashes, frozen screens and menus that stopped answering, from a review of the front end.
- Saves, settings, shares and the updater are written so that an interrupted write loses nothing.
- `5443000D`'s 120 fps mod (hakuX's) is withdrawn: with it on, the game stopped at the end of the
  first chapter's opening scene. Consoles that have it get an empty one through the game-fixes
  download, so the mod does nothing even when it is on.

## Tested
On a PS5, from test builds of this version:
- `54540079`: races past the point where it froze (test build EH).
- `4D530004`: the water's reflection; `4D53000D`: hair, light and the pond; `5553001D`: no halo;
  `4D530039`: cube reflections (test build EN).
- `5553001D` works as it should (test builds EJ and EM).
- `5443000D`: its cache drive reused on a second start (test build EJ's log), and the game going on

- On the PC: every PC test (`tests/run-pc-tests.sh --fetch`), among them the new test of the cheats
  download; hakuX-native's tests (`tests/hle`, its 31 test programs; `tests/cohalias`, 156 runs;
  `tests/coh16k`) and its new save-state cache test (`tests/statecache`).

The fixes ported from xbased and HakuXP were not each tested on the console.

## Installation
1. Download `OGBox-v1.0.3.zip` below and check it against `OGBox-v1.0.3.zip.sha256`.
2. Copy the `PPSA99251` folder in it to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, replacing the old one.
   0.0.2 and newer also offer this release at their next start (Settings > Updates). Your
   settings, saves, save states and caches stay where they are (`/data/ogbox`, or the data folder
   you chose).
3. Put your own disc images (or extracted games) on the console, a USB drive, extended storage or
   a network share. Read `LEGAL.txt` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
Open an issue on this repository. Before you report a game's problem, please:
1. Use a dump of your own disc that matches Redump (the names in OGBox's game database are
   Redump's). A bad or modified dump causes faults OGBox can't fix.
2. Try the game with every mod, cheat or trainer and XBE patch turned off (and default settings,
   if you can), and say whether it still happens.
3. Include:
   - `ogbox-boot.log` from the title folder (choose **Save the Log** in the quick menu first if it
     wasn't a crash; after a crash, copy it and `ogbox-boot.previous.log` before you start OGBox
     again);
   - the game's **Title ID** as its page shows it (with the version after the dot, as in
     `4D530039.1`);
   - your OGBox version and your firmware version;
   - what you did.

Never attach or link game files, BIOS files or keys.
