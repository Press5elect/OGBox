# OGBox v0.0.4

An unofficial port of the hakuX original Xbox emulator to the PS5, as a homebrew title
(`PPSA99251`) for a jailbroken console.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Piracy is not condoned.** This release contains no games, no Xbox BIOS, boot ROM, EEPROM,
> firmware or keys, and none will ever be provided or linked to. Use only **backups of games you
> own**, made yourself from your own discs.

> [!WARNING]
> **Highly experimental.** Expect crashes and freezes. This is my build of hakuX for the PS5, not
> the hakuX project's: please report problems here, not to hakuX.

## Added
- Settings > Anisotropic filtering (2x to 16x), Sharpening and Force 32-bit colour. All off by
  default: the picture stays the Xbox's own.

## Changed
- The emulated console's refresh follows the PS5's display, so a finished frame no longer lands
  on the moment the screen takes one.
- Frames go from the emulated GPU straight into the picture, with no copy by the processor.
- The sound's thread has a processor core of its own.
- Sound is always mixed the fast way, as in xemu's default. Full DSP processing is experimental
  in xemu too (some games' sound programs never finish a frame), so its option is hidden.
- Covers are kept under the game's file name, and a picture of your own with that name wins.
- Test builds say "developer build" beside their version.

## Fixed
- Frames shown twice or skipped in games running at 60 fps, most at high resolution scales.
- Movies stuttering while a game loads behind them (opening a file read the disc's folders from
  the network share each time).
- A black screen in the Xbox dashboard at a resolution scale of 5 or 6.
- OGBox not closing after the Xbox dashboard.
- Covers filling in one by one after coming back from a game.
- An `env` line first in `boot.txt` being taken for the disc image.

## Installation
1. Download `OGBox-v0.0.4.zip` below and check it against `OGBox-v0.0.4.zip.sha256`.
2. Copy the `PPSA99251` folder in it to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, replacing the old one.
   0.0.2 and newer also offer this release at its next start (Settings > Updates). Your settings, saves,
   save states and caches stay (`/data/ogbox`, or the data folder you chose).
3. Put your own disc images on the console, a USB drive, extended storage or a network share, and
   read `LEGAL.txt` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
Open an issue on this repository with `ogbox-boot.log` and `ogbox-boot.previous.log` from the
title folder (copy them before you start OGBox again), your firmware version and what you did.
Never attach or link game files, BIOS files or keys.
