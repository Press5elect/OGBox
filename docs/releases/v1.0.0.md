## Added
- **60 FPS patches of OGBox's own** (a game's Patches, off until you turn them on) for title IDs
  `45410012`, `4D5300D1`, `55530041`, `4D53000D`, `54540008`, `54540079` (versions 1, 2, 5, 6),
  `41560020` (versions 3, 5) and `4156004E`. Each keeps the game's speed and plays its videos at
  their own rate. Patches that place code check the XBE's CRC-32 first.
- **120 fps mods** for a 120 Hz screen: `4E4D0009` (version 3, menus at 60), `55530041`
  (version 2) and `45410083` (version 2). They join `54540082`'s and `545400A4`'s from before.
  To use one, set Settings > Graphics > Display refresh rate to 120 Hz and turn the 60 fps patch
  or mod off. `45410083` also gets its full 60 fps mod: physics, ragdolls, recoil, sway, zoom and
  glass run at the speed they have at 30. A mod's author shows on the game page.
- **HDR** (Settings > Picture and Sound): the picture goes to an HDR TV as HDR10 (BT.2020, PQ).
  The game's brightest parts are raised toward HDR highlights (Off, or 400 to 4000 nits).
  **Adjust HDR brightness** is a test screen for the TV's peak and the white level.
- **Brightness, Contrast and Colour** per game, changed at once from the quick menu, in SDR and
  HDR.
- **Quincunx anti-aliasing**, the original Xbox's own.
- **SSAA + SMAA**, and SSAA's Sharp (Lanczos) down-sampling.
- **Debanding**, **Texture sharpness** and the **CRT look**.
- **Internal resolution up to 12x.** Above 6x needs Settings > Advanced > Internal resolutions
  above 6x, because performance may vary and it may crash.
- **A startup video**: the OGBox X cracks and shatters black glass, with its own sound. It lasts
  8 seconds; CROSS or CIRCLE skips it, and Settings > Interface > Startup video turns it off.
- **DualSense in the menus** (Settings > Interface > DualSense): Vibration, Circle confirms, and
  the light bar.
- **A safe exit.** Going back to the game list, a restart, a state load or closing OGBox first
  waits for what is still on its way to the disk, with a bar at the bottom saying what.
- **A USB drive for data.** Choosing where to keep data lists a USB drive first, and recommends
  it. The console slows a title's writes to its own storage after a few GB, which slows the game;
  on a USB drive it doesn't.
- **Save states can be deleted** (TRIANGLE), "where you left off" too.
- **Network shares are woken when OGBox starts**, so a NAS's disks are turning by the time a game
  starts.
- **Cache drives' writes** are held in memory and written behind the game.

## Fixed
- Stencil shadows drawn as wedges, spikes and flickering patches (`41560020`). Exact depth now
  takes a primitive clipped at the eye from clip space.
- A hang a few hundred frames in at 720p (`4E4D0003`). The scanline register now counts the video
  mode's lines.
- Save states that didn't load ("too many open files"), and the quick menu's apply with them.
- The movies of the `54540082` and `545400A4` 120 fps mods played twice as fast.
- A game that jumps to an address with nothing there now stops with a report, instead of closing
  OGBox.
- Kernel fixes, checked against Cxbx-Reloaded's kernel test suite (its expected results come from a
  console). 80 of its 87 tests with checks pass; before, it stopped at its second test.
  - About 30 kernel functions that stopped a game now exist: thread creation, string and number
    routines, stack walks, kernel queues, I/O completion ports, directory objects, raised statuses
    and `XcPKDecPrivate`.
  - Where results differed from the console's, they now match: Latin-1 case, string conversion
    limits, DES and RSA edge cases, section page counts and suspend counts.
- A thread's end sets what `GetExitCodeThread` reads, so games that wait for one of their threads
  no longer wait forever.
- The AV pack is now the one the console reports (`4D530064` lost 480p).
- Code a game rewrites and then protects now runs as rewritten.
- A critical section that is never initialized no longer hangs.
- MMX instructions with a `0x66` prefix now run correctly (a crash at boot).
- Vertex alignment on the PS5's GPU, and three-component vertex attributes.
- Higher limits: XBE sections, nested exceptions, and contiguous memory.
- Directory listings with FindFirstFile's dot rules, and named kernel objects.
- GPU work queued into memory a game had freed.
- Merged from hakuX-lr (PortareOS):
  - kernel calls' faults go to the game's exception handlers;
  - NV097's inline attribute methods;
  - the pipeline table is recycled when it fills;
  - 2D blits are clipped as the NV2A does;
  - render targets across adjacent blocks of memory;
  - the DSP's loops that end in a call.
- Textures drawn from render targets of other sizes and layouts.
- Frame generation turning itself off after a change from the quick menu.

## Tested
On a PS5, from test builds of this version:
- 60 FPS patches:
  - `41560020` version 3: about 59.3 fps, with its shadows right;
  - `54540079` version 5: a median of 59.9 fps;
  - `45410012`: 60 fps;
  - `55530041`: works, with its videos at their own rate.
- 120 fps mods on a 120 Hz screen:
  - `4E4D0009`: 119.9 fps in play;
  - `55530041`: 117.8 to 119.9 fps;
  - `54540082`: 119.9 fps;
  - `45410083`: its first level, about 4 minutes at a mean of 119.9 fps, never below 100, no
    faults.
- The `41560020` shadow fix and the `4E4D0003` 720p fix.
- The DualSense menu settings.
- The data folder on a USB drive against the console's own storage: no slowdowns on the USB drive.
- A large set of games on the kernel and GPU test builds, and Cxbx-Reloaded's kernel test suite.

## Installation
1. Download `OGBox-v1.0.0.zip` below and check it against `OGBox-v1.0.0.zip.sha256`.
2. Copy the `PPSA99251` folder in it to `/data/homebrew/` on the console, replacing the old one.
   0.0.2 and newer also offer this release at their next start (Settings > Updates). Your
   settings, saves, save states and caches stay where they are (`/data/ogbox`, or the data folder
   you chose).
3. Put your own disc images (or extracted games) on the console, a USB drive, extended storage or
   a network share. Read `LEGAL.txt` in the folder.

## Reporting problems
Open an issue on this repository and include:
- `ogbox-boot.log` and `ogbox-boot.previous.log` from the title folder (copy them before you
  start OGBox again);
- your firmware version;
- what you did.

If the problem isn't a crash, choose **Save the Log** in the quick menu first. Never attach or
link game files, BIOS files or keys.
